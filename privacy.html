Pichani is a private photo library. This notice explains what we collect, why, where it is kept, and what you can do about it. The operator of Pichani is [BUSINESS NAME AND REGISTERED ADDRESS]. Questions about this notice go to [CONTACT EMAIL].

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, your password (stored by our authentication provider in protected form, never in readable form), and an optional display name.
  • Your photos: the image files you upload, so that you can view, download and organise them.
  • Photo details: file name, file type, file size, the date the photo was taken or last modified on your device, and whether you have marked it as a favourite.
  • Albums: album names, descriptions and which photos belong to each album.
  • Technical data: the signed-in session your browser keeps so that you stay logged in, and the usual server logs that record requests.

Why we collect it

To run your account, store and show your photos, enforce the storage limits of your plan, let you recover your password, and keep the service secure. We do not sell your information and do not use your photos for advertising.

Who can see your photos

Only you can see your photos and albums. Each photo is stored in a private area that is reachable only through your signed-in account. Pichani does not currently offer sharing with other people.

Where your information is stored

Account details, photo details and the photo files are stored with Supabase, which provides our database, authentication and file storage. The website itself is served by GitHub Pages. Your data is stored in the Supabase region [REGION].

Other services the site contacts

  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces used on the site.
  • jsDelivr serves the Supabase library the site needs to run.
  • Unsplash serves a decorative image on the home page. Loading it sends your IP address to Unsplash.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and photos for as long as your account exists. When you delete a photo, it is removed from your library. [ACCOUNT DELETION PROCESS AND TIMESCALE] Backups held by our providers may keep copies for a limited period [BACKUP RETENTION].

Your choices

  • Access and export: Settings → Export downloads a list of your albums and photo details. Select photos on All photos to download the pictures themselves.
  • Correct your name: Settings → Edit profile.
  • Change your password: Settings → Change password.
  • Delete your data: [DELETION INSTRUCTIONS]. Until an in-app option is available, email [CONTACT EMAIL] from the address registered to your account.

Security

Photo files are kept in a private storage area, and database rules limit each account to its own records. No online service is completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Changes to this notice
