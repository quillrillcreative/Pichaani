Getting started

How do I create an account?

Tap Sign in, then Create account. Enter your name, email and a password of at least 8 characters. If we ask you to confirm your email, open the link we send you, then sign in.

I forgot my password. What now?

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password? and enter your email. Open the link in the email and choose a new password.

Can I use Pichani on my phone?

Yes. Pichani works in your phone’s browser, in portrait or landscape.

Photos

How do I upload photos?

Sign in, then tap Upload photos. You can select several photos at once. Each upload shows its progress, and you can retry any that fail.

Which photo formats are supported?

JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, HEIC, HEIF and AVIF. Each file can be up to 50 MB. Some browsers can’t show HEIC or HEIF photos, but they are still stored safely and you can download them.

How do I download or delete a photo?

Tap a photo to open it, then use Download or Delete. To work on several photos at once, tap Select on any photo page, tick the photos, and choose an action.

Who can see my photos?

Only you. Pichani does not share your photos with anyone. Read the privacy notice for details.

Albums and favourites

How do I make an album?

Open Albums and tap New album. To add photos, open All photos, tap Select, tick the photos and choose Move.

How do I favourite a photo?

Tap the heart on a photo, or use Favourite when the photo is open. Your favourites appear under Favourites.

Storage and plans

How much can I store?

The Free plan includes up to 5 GB of storage and 1,500 photos, whichever you reach first. Your usage is shown in Settings and in Explore plans.

Are there paid plans?

Paid plans are not priced yet. You won’t be charged for anything today.

Your data

How do I export my data?

Open Settings and tap Export. You get a file listing your albums and photo details. To download the pictures themselves, use Select on All photos.

How do I delete my account?

Account deletion isn’t available in the app yet. Email [CONTACT EMAIL] from the address on your account and we’ll delete it.
